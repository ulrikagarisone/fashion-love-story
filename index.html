THE LOVE STORY

One designer's vision.
The story that changed fashion.

In the 1980s, fashion and sports lived on different planets.

Fashion lived on runways and red carpets. It was about exclusivity, craftsmanship and materials that had been perfected over centuries. Meanwhile, sports occupied a completely different universe—one of function, performance, and cutting-edge synthetic materials designed for one purpose: to help athletes move faster, jump higher, push harder.

At that time, the world of sports and the world of fashion were two different planets, with apparently nothing in common. Until I—the Matchmaker—changed the rules forever.

Fashion shows:
Leather everywhere.
Lycra? Never.

Sports stadiums:
Lycra everywhere.
Leather? Too stiff.

THE LEATHER YEARS

This was my breakthrough innovation - laces that wrapped through the actual soles, never seen before in footwear. This technique became my signature.

late 80's

Brown tall boots front wrapped by laces through the soles

Military precision in every design. Italian craftsmanship in every detail. Sculptural back panel became iconic. Bold proportions. Statement hardware. Metal accents that meant rebellion. This defined my rebellious DNA.

1988

Perfecto leather jacket with metal details

I brought street functionality to the runway. Heavy-duty soles with purpose. Metal hardware that meant business. My signature was written in bold hardware and rebellion.

1989

Military combat boots with metal hardware

I transformed ordinary pants into functional armor. Zippers placed unconventionally—function over form. Strategic placement. Deliberate detail. This was fashion that meant business.

Early 1990s

All around zipper leather pants

I deconstructed the biker jacket. Straps and buckles repositioned strategically. I didn't follow tradition—I questioned it. Structure became my rebellious tool. The jacket transformed into sculptural armor.

1992

Biker jacket with multiple straps and buckles

My signature lacing evolved. Now through the heel. Threading boundaries of what footwear could be. I pushed technical limits with every stitch. Function merged with form in ways never seen. The heel became sculptural, purposeful, rebellious.

1993

Lace through heel boots

All signature elements merged into one. Military flaps. Strategic heel lacing. An aggressive silhouette that meant business. This was peak leather craftsmanship. The boot had become revolutionary.

1994

Black boots with flap and laces through the heel

Mastery. Complete. My final statement in pure leather. Before Lycra. Leather. Metal. Rebellion. Perfected. Crystallized. Undeniable. 1995 marked the end of one era. And the beginning of everything that would follow.

Circa 2000.
Disaster in Milan. My model fell ill. I had the clothes, but no one to fill them.

Desperate, we pulled a stockroom worker with similar measurements. He wasn't just an employee; he was a captain.

02

That stockroom worker wasn't just any employee.

He was the captain of FC Fossombrone, a small-town Italian football club. A real athlete. Testing real designs.

I started picking clothes based on his reactions. He showed me the truth:

  • Too much Leather? He can't perform.
  • Too much Lycra? He feels naked.
