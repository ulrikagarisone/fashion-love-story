THE LOVE STORY

One designer's vision.
The story that changed fashion.

In the 1980s, fashion and sports lived on different planets.

Fashion lived on runways and red carpets. It was about exclusivity, craftsmanship and materials that had been perfected over centuries. Meanwhile, sports occupied a completely different universe—one of function, performance, and cutting-edge synthetic materials designed for one purpose: to help athletes move faster, jump higher, push harder.

At that time, the world of sports and the world of fashion were two different planets, with apparently nothing in common. Until I—the Matchmaker—changed the rules forever.

Fashion shows:
Leather everywhere.
Lycra? Never.

Sports stadiums:
Lycra everywhere.
Leather? Too stiff.

THE FAILED DATES

THE SUFFOCATING ROMANCE

In the late 1990s, I strayed from who I was.

I was the guy known for tough tailoring and rugged boots, but I tried to be someone else. I launched a women's collection. On the surface, it was a perfect match. Elegant. Refined. But deep down? It felt like a lie.

LEATHER:

"I wanted to impress. I wore my finest. Metallic accessories. Delicate lattice work. Everything elegant. Everything refined."

LYCRA:

"But there's no room for me here. It's too... stiff. Too precious. I can't breathe. I can't move."

Leather had tried to be someone else.
And lycra? Lycra wasn't even in the picture yet.

Undeniable craft,
